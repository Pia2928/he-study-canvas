Your mission

Design and develop one small entrepreneurial project that you could realistically pursue on the side, next to your studies or your job. Show who it is for, what you offer and how you would get started.

How it works

  1. Register your idea. Describe it below. It can still be rough.
  2. Build your plan. On the next page you are guided through the nine boxes of a one-page project map.
  3. Open for business. When you finish, or when the time is up, your plan becomes a first version of your project's website, and you continue to a short survey.

Who can join

Anyone who has an idea for a small project to start on the side. You take part on your own, on a laptop or desktop computer.

Choose a small project you would realistically pursue on the side. It does not have to be perfect.
