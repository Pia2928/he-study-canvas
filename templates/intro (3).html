Your mission

Take one small entrepreneurial project that you would like to pursue on the side, next to your studies or your job, and develop it into a plan that you could start acting on.

How it works

  1. Describe your idea. It can still be rough.
  2. Build your plan on a map. Nine short boxes help you think through the important parts of your project, such as who pays, what you do and how customers find you.
  3. Open for business. When you finish, or when the time is up, your plan becomes a first version of your project's website, and you continue to a short survey.

Choose a small project you would realistically pursue on the side. It does not have to be perfect.
